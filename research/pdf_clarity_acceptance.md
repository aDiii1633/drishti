# PDF clarity acceptance evidence

An authenticated administrator uploaded a temporary eight-page booklet and a temporary question paper to the live Scan Intake route. The live interface reported **Batch 8/8** and **All 8 pages passed the clarity gate**. The evidence gallery displayed page cards with rendered page images, CLEAR labels, and variance measurements, including cards beyond pages 5 and 6 in the scrollable gallery.

This confirms that the client-side loop reaches the final page and progressively exposes the generated preview evidence rather than presenting a fixed or truncated page count.
